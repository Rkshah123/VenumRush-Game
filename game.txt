# 3. TRUE 3D SNAKE GAMEPLAY

This must be a genuinely three-dimensional game.

## Snake construction

Build snakes using procedural geometry, reusable 3D segments, a tube mesh, or another efficient method that produces a continuous, rounded body.

Each snake should have:

- A distinctive head
- Expressive eyes
- A mouth or stylized facial details
- A smooth neck transition
- A flexible body with many visible segments
- A tapered tail
- Material variation
- Individual markings or scales
- Emissive accents
- Smooth movement and turning
- A visible growth response when consuming food

The body must follow the head's movement naturally.

Use a robust body-following system based on sampled path positions, a distance-spaced point history, or a suitable spline-based technique.

Maintain consistent segment spacing while the snake turns.

Prevent body jitter, segment overlap, sudden teleportation, visible gaps, and excessive stretching.

The snake must not behave like a rigid chain of unrelated spheres.

Provide a procedural fallback if external 3D models are unavailable. The game must remain playable without paid assets or inaccessible downloads.

## Movement

Implement:

- Smooth acceleration and deceleration
- Adjustable movement speed
- Responsive steering
- Natural turning
- A configurable turning radius
- Head tilt during turns
- Body lag and follow-through
- Subtle idle movement
- Animated tongue or head details where appropriate
- Temporary speed boosts
- Recovery after boosting
- Camera smoothing
- Reliable collision detection

Use frame-rate-independent movement and stable simulation steps where needed.

Support keyboard, mouse, and touch controls without requiring different gameplay rules for each platform.

## Growth

Food consumption must have immediate visual and gameplay consequences.

When the player eats:

- Increase score.
- Increase length.
- Add or redistribute body segments.
- Update the length indicator.
- Play a satisfying sound.
- Trigger a brief visual effect.
- Spawn a small particle burst.
- Produce a subtle camera response.
- Update any relevant achievements or mission progress.

Growth must be bounded by performance safeguards without making long snakes visually broken.

Create configurable minimum and maximum length values.

## Collision and elimination

Implement consistent collision rules for:

- Snake heads
- Snake bodies
- Food
- Arena boundaries
- Hazards
- Enemy attacks or special abilities

Use appropriate spatial partitioning or collision-query optimization for large numbers of segments.

Define the rules explicitly.

For example, a head-first collision with another snake's body may eliminate the attacking snake, while contact with food grants growth.

Allow special combat abilities to modify specific collision outcomes, but never create arbitrary, unexplained invulnerability.

On elimination:

1. Stop the eliminated snake's movement.
2. Trigger a short, readable death animation.
3. Convert part of its mass into collectible energy orbs.
4. Play the elimination sound.
5. Update scores and kill statistics.
6. Inform the HUD.
7. Show the game-over screen when the player dies.
8. Allow immediate restart or return to the menu.

All collision and elimination rules must be testable and consistent.
